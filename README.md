Disruptive technologies are innovative inventions or applications that fundamentally change the way industries and society operate, often by offering simpler, more affordable, or entirely new ways to meet customer needs, eventually displacing established approaches. Data science is the process of using scientific methods, algorithms, and systems to extract valuable insights, knowledge, and actionable patterns from large volumes of data (Big Data).
